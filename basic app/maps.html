Google Maps enables exploration and navigation with detailed maps, satellite imagery, and Street View. It offers real-time traffic, transit schedules, and route planning for driving, walking, biking, and public transit to help users reach destinations efficiently.

Developers can integrate Maps APIs to add location-aware features, place search, and interactive map experiences into their applications.
