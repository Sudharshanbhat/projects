Google Drive

Google Drive is a cloud storage and collaboration platform that lets users store files securely, share with others, and access documents from any device. Drive integrates with Docs, Sheets, and Slides for seamless real-time collaboration.

Features include file version history, granular sharing controls, offline access, and powerful search to find content quickly across files and owners.
